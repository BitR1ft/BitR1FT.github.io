A brief overview of who I am and what I do

I am a cybersecurity undergraduate at Air University, Islamabad (NCSA Department), currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security (BSCYS). My focus lies at the intersection of offensive security and defensive operations, with deep interests in penetration testing, detection engineering, and real-world vulnerability research.

I recently completed a VAPT internship at the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA), where I conducted manual penetration tests on live government web applications, identified critical vulnerabilities including SSRF and IDOR, and delivered CVSS-scored assessment reports with full proof-of-concept exploit chains.

I spend most of my time doing bug bounty research and competing in CTFs under the handle BitR1FT. My project work spans both attacking and defending — from autonomous pentesting platforms and post-quantum VPNs to self-hosted SIEMs with custom Sigma detections and threat intel integration.
